# NaNa Law Library — Germany

Country repository for original-language German legislation, connected to the
[NaNa Law Library](https://nanalab.kr/world-law-portal/).

This repository is being prepared. No legal documents have been published from
this repository yet. The existing portal remains available at its current URLs.

New documents will be grouped by their verified official legal type. German acts
and regulations will retain their original titles and text. No translation of
legislation, PDF downloads, images, OCR, or private legal commentary is provided.

Each published document must identify its official source, edition, scope,
reuse basis, applicable conditions, and transformation notice. Original legal
texts and site code have separate rights; this repository does not claim a
blanket licence over third-party material.

Only independently checked, explicitly approved source batches may be published.
Collection counts do not establish national completeness or current legal force.
